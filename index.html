Research Problem Statement

Existing deepfake detection methods often rely heavily on facial appearance, image artifacts and expression inconsistencies. As deepfake generation becomes more realistic, these visible cues may become less reliable. Therefore, a robust detection approach is needed that investigates complementary signals from facial physiology and temporal behavior—such as rPPG-derived pulse information, blinking patterns and subtle facial micro-movements—to improve deepfake identification.

DeepDetect Methodology & Science

How physiological biosignals expose generative synthesis where visual cues fail.

1

Facial Alignment & Skin ROIs

Extracts face bounding boxes and isolates the forehead and cheek capillary beds where dermis blood perfusion is most uninhibited by facial expressions.

2

Motion Stability Score

A Convolutional Attention Network processes appearance frames to generate dynamic skin attention masks while computing motion difference frames (dA/dt).

3

Chrominance rPPG & FFT

Translates RGB micro-fluctuations into Blood Volume Pulses using CHROM & POS projection. Fourier analysis computes heart rate peak and Signal-to-Noise Ratio.

4

Multi-Signal Fusion Decision

Combines BVP pulse periodicity, Eye Aspect Ratio blink frequencies, and optical micro-movement stability to produce a rule-based authenticity index and synthetic risk score.
